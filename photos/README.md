## What I left out

Some of the photos I received turned out to be pictures of **pages from a printed reference workbook**, not my own work — for example, pages titled "카운터 선택 표시 회로" and "전자 사이크로" share titles with exercises already in `circuit-design-problem-set`, but these particular photos show numbered pages (135, 147) with tab markers, consistent with a published/distributed textbook scan rather than a personal notebook. One photo of an official problem PDF displayed on a monitor was excluded for the same reason — it's a reproduction of copyrighted original text.
